# v0.3 pane pictures

Drawn by `PaneHarness.exe` on a GitHub `windows-latest` runner (CI run 36523247898, commit 8a06121) from the real `UnstuckPane` with made-up SOLIDWORKS states. No SOLIDWORKS was involved. There are seven states (first run, empty, typing, answer, right now, practice, no document) at pane widths 260, 320 and 420 and scales 100, 125 and 150 percent. The 125 and 150 percent scales are simulated: layout and fonts are multiplied by the scale, not produced by a real Windows DPI change. There are also high-contrast answer and right-now pictures, and an answer at A++. `harness-report.json` has the per-picture checks (all 66: no horizontal scroll bar, no control wider than the pane, no button under 36 px, no stale layout) and the timings. `compare-v0.2-v0.3.png` puts Mr. Pina's v0.2 screenshot beside the v0.3 "right now" state.

**What a student now sees.** In v0.2, a student opening SolidWorks saw a column of eight identical gray buttons (Find my part, Set units, Exit sketch, Roll to end, Undo...), then a practice checklist with font-fallback squares and a "Do it for me" button under nearly every step. Nothing said where to start, and there was no icon to find the pane by. In v0.3 the pane has a green magnifier icon and a dark title bar like SolidWorks. The first thing under the title is a search box that says "Type what's happening, in your own words". When SolidWorks shows something the helper is sure about, one white card says it in one sentence ("You are in millimeters.") with one blue button to fix it; otherwise the card is not there. Below are six common problems as full-width rows tagged "Something is wrong", then a closed "Browse all answers". Typing shows up to five matching rows as you type. Opening one replaces the list with the answer: the fix in bold, numbered steps each tagged with where to look ("Left panel", "Top menu", "Keyboard"), and "Did that fix it?" with Yes and Not yet. "Read it to me" and a small "Copy for AI" link come last. Practice moved to its own tab with real, green-ticked check boxes, one path open at a time, and "Do it for me" demoted to a small link.

Known cosmetic issue: at A++ the first answer step can have one extra blank line under it. It shows in the harness picture, but no layout mismatch was detected. The likely cause is text drawn into a bitmap measuring slightly differently from text drawn on screen, but that is not confirmed.
