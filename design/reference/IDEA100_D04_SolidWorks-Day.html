▾ Wrench

    Break it on purpose

    Cause each problem, then fix it using Unstuck, not a TA.

    1. Leave the sketch, get back in

    Break it: Edit Sketch1, then click Exit Sketch.

    2. A shape that is not closed

    Break it: Sketch on the front face. Draw three lines that do not close. Try Extruded Cut.

    3. The rollback bar

    Break it: Drag the yellow and black bar in the tree up above Cut-Extrude1. The hex disappears.

    4. Millimeters

    Break it: Click IPS at the bottom right and pick MMGS. Look at the numbers.

    5. A red sketch

    Break it: Edit Sketch1. Dimension center to center again. If a box asks, pick Leave this dimension driving.
