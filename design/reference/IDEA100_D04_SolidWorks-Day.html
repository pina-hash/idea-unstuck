    Your hook

    Start from your own paper sketch. No demo: use the recipe, the map and Unstuck.4 pts
    1. Pick one of your paper sketches from Hook 01. Find its two circles: the hole at the top and the curve at the bottom.Look at: the big part area
    2. New part, IPS. Ctrl+S: V1_ and your own last name. Two-piece hook? Name the first piece V1A_ and your last name.Look at: your keyboard
    3. Front Plane, Sketch. Draw the two circles and size them: top hole 1.000 or bigger, bottom inside 1.000 or bigger, centers 2.000 to 3.000 apart.Look at: the big part area
    4. Draw the outside around them: bigger circles and lines (the wrench), lines and arcs with A (drill 1), or a path and Offset (drill 2).Look at: the big part area
    5. Trim to one outline. Keep the opening at least 0.500. Extrude, 0.750 or thinner.Look at: the tool buttons under the tabs
    6. Not done? That is fine. Ctrl+S, hand in what you have, and say what stopped you. You keep building it tomorrow.Look at: your keyboard
    Stuck?

    Hand in your hook V1_LASTNAME.SLDPRT

    Drop or choose your .SLDPRT
    Press Ctrl+S in SolidWorks first. Not finished is fine.

    More ways to break it

    Optional. Cause each problem, then fix it using Unstuck, not a TA.

    1. The rollback bar

    Break it: Drag the yellow and black bar in the tree up above Fillet1. The rounded edges disappear.

    2. Millimeters

    Break it: Click IPS at the bottom right and pick MMGS. Look at the numbers.

    3. A red sketch

    Break it: Edit Sketch1. Dimension center to center again. If a box asks, pick Leave this dimension driving.
